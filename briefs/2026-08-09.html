Custody & Security🔴
BTCPay restricts remote Lightning access after attackers steal funds
Why it matters to BOF: the BTCPay Server flaw disclosed Friday has now claimed named victims — Foundation Devices (maker of the Passport hardware wallet) confirmed its Lightning node was drained overnight, and bitcoin publication Citadel21 reported a similar sweep. The bug let unauthenticated attackers steal LND ".macaroon" credential files remotely and seize Lightning channels; BTCPay's on-chain wallets were not affected. BTCPay's v2.4.2 patch stops new access but does not invalidate credentials already stolen — any BOF portfolio company or counterparty running a BTCPay-managed Lightning node needs to confirm both the patch AND a full LND macaroon rotation, not just the update. This is now the fourth named casualty in a single week of bitcoin-infrastructure exploits (Coldcard, the original BTCPay disclosure, and two named Lightning-node victims).
Broke: overnight — Cointelegraph's report on BTCPay's access restriction published 1:46am ET; Foundation and Citadel21 confirmed their nodes swept overnight into Sunday
