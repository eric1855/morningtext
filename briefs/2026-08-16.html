Bitcoin Miners & Infrastructure🟡
Why the world's second-largest Bitcoin mining power is shutting down rigs in its capital city
Why it matters to BOF: Russia's Energy Ministry has barred crypto mining in Moscow, the wider Moscow Region, and parts of Kursk through December 31, 2032, citing power-capacity risk as data-center demand competing for the same grid could reach 3.6 GW (17% of peak regional demand) by 2032. The read for BOF's distressed-miner-debt book: this is a multi-year regulatory overhang on Russian mining capacity, a jurisdiction-risk data point worth pricing into any exposure to Russia-domiciled or -adjacent mining credits.
