Coldcard self-custody disaster nearly doubles to $70M — forensics show the attack never touched a single physical device
Why it matters to BOF: Galaxy Research now maps 1,082.65 BTC (~$70M) swept from 1,196 wallets in a 41-minute window on July 30, up from the ~$38M first reported yesterday. The mechanism is the more important update: the March-2021 Coldcard Mk3 firmware bug produced predictable seeds from non-secret chip data, so the attacker reconstructed private keys entirely offline against the public chain — the victim's hardware could be powered off in a safe on another continent. That breaks the "cold storage = safe" assumption BOF's custody policy likely leans on for any Mk3-era hardware; treat this as a firmware-audit and key-rotation trigger, not a watch item. CoinDesk separately reports the shock may accelerate flows from self-custody into ETF wrappers — a structural tailwind for the products BOF already holds, and worth tracking in Monday's flow data.
