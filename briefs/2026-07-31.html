Custody & Security🔴
Coldcard firmware flaw drains 594 BTC ($38M) from ~500 wallets in a 25-minute sweep
Why it matters to BOF: a predictable-RNG bug introduced in Coldcard firmware 4.0.0 (Mk3 devices, since March 2021) let an attacker rebuild private keys from non-secret device data and sweep ~500 single-sig wallets before anyone could react. Mk4/Q/Mk5 appear unaffected, but this is a direct hit to the "hardware wallet = safe self-custody" assumption that underpins a lot of cold-storage policy in this space. If BOF or any LP/counterparty holds keys generated on affected Mk3 firmware, treat this as an immediate key-rotation trigger, not a watch item.
