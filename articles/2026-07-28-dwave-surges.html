D-Wave surges 20% on expanded AT&T quantum-network deal

Equities · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • Quantum computing name D-Wave jumped roughly 20% Monday after announcing an expanded partnership with AT&T for quantum network optimization, per Yahoo Finance and The Motley Fool.
  • The move came the same day chip stocks broadly sold off, making D-Wave one of the few notable single-stock winners in an otherwise risk-off session for tech hardware.
  • The deal underscores continued corporate appetite for quantum-adjacent infrastructure spend even as sentiment on traditional AI-chip capex sours.
  • Why it matters: a bright spot in an otherwise ugly tape for tech hardware — worth watching whether capital rotates from crowded AI-chip names into adjacent quantum/infrastructure plays if the semiconductor selloff persists.
Sources: Yahoo Finance, The Motley Fool.
