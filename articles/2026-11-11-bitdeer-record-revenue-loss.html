Bitdeer Reports Record Q3 Revenue as Net Loss Widens to $125.5 Million

Bitcoin Miners & Infrastructure / Earnings · Tuesday, November 10, 2026, ~9:26am ET
  • Bitcoin miner Bitdeer reported record quarterly revenue for Q3 2026, even as its net loss widened to $125.5 million.
  • The divergence between record top-line growth and a widening bottom-line loss is consistent with the sector's AI/HPC diversification push: revenue growth from new business lines isn't yet offsetting the cost of the pivot or bitcoin-mining margin pressure.
  • Why it matters to BOF: useful underwriting context for distinguishing miners whose AI/HPC pivots are becoming self-funding from those still burning cash and relying on debt or equity issuance to bridge the gap.
