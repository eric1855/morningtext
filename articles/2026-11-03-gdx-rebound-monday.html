GDX claws back over half of Friday's washout, +2.67% to $79.15 Monday

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Monday, November 2, 2026 (reported into Tuesday morning)
  • The VanEck Gold Miners ETF (GDX) closed Monday at $79.15, up $2.06, or 2.67%, from Friday's $77.09 close — recovering a little over half of Friday's 5.21% single-session washout, with an intraday range of $78.62-$80.41.
  • The move tracked Monday's broader metals bounce (spot gold +1.84% to ~$3,945.80, spot silver +3.67% to ~$50.80) at roughly 1.4-1.5x the metals' own gain — a healthy, not excessive, leverage multiple for the producer/developer cohort EMA's book holds, in contrast to Friday's outsized downside multiple.
  • Overnight into Tuesday, the setup reversed again: a reported (CENTCOM-unconfirmed) U.S. strike on Iran's Qeshm Island pushed oil higher and pulled spot gold back down to roughly $3,920 (-0.67%) and silver toward $49, undoing part of the diplomacy-driven rally that drove Monday's miner bounce.
  • Why it matters to EMA: GDX is the fund's own benchmark, so whether Monday's gain holds at Tuesday's open — against overnight weakness in the metals themselves — is the first real test of whether Friday's washout was a fair-value reset (durable bounce) or an overshoot in both directions (round-trip back toward Friday's lows).
Sources: VanEck, Yahoo Finance, Investing.com, Heygotrade, Kitco.
