US pauses new Iran strikes at Tehran's request, but pause stays fragile

Geopolitical · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • President Trump said the US halted new strikes on Iran at the regime's request, following nearly two weeks of intensified strikes, per CNN's live coverage.
  • Trump warned attacks would resume if the two sides fail to reach a new ceasefire deal; Iran has publicly denied agreeing to any 10-day ceasefire, per CNN.
  • Reports that US munitions stockpiles are running low are reportedly factoring into the administration's military calculus, per CNBC.
  • The pause held through Monday despite the denial, though CNBC describes it as a fragile moment in an escalation that has cycled through multiple failed ceasefire attempts since spring.
  • Separately, Oman has presented Iran with a proposal for a regional mechanism to manage Strait of Hormuz shipping, an effort still in early stages.
  • Why it matters: the fragile pause underpins the recent easing in oil and yields — any breakdown would quickly reverse both, and remains the single biggest tail risk hanging over this week's markets.
Sources: CNN, CNBC, Wikipedia (2026 Iran war ceasefire).
