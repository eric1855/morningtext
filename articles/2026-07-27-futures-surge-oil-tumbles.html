Futures surge as oil sinks, busiest week of the quarter ahead

Markets · Monday, July 27, 2026
  • Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 and Dow futures were up roughly 1.6%, 1.0% and 1.0% respectively as of pre-market Monday, per TipRanks and Yahoo Finance.
  • The lift traces directly to the US-Iran de-escalation and the accompanying crude sell-off — Brent futures traded below $86-90/barrel at last check, down sharply from Friday's spike levels.
  • A stronger-than-expected Services PMI print (53.6) added to the constructive tape, signaling travel, events and business-services activity is reaccelerating.
  • Investors are entering the busiest week of the quarter: four of the "Magnificent Seven" report earnings (Apple, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft), the Fed delivers its rate decision Wednesday, and Core PCE lands Thursday.
  • Why it matters: today's gap-up is a relief rally unwinding two weeks of war-risk pricing — but it is fragile. Any reversal in the ceasefire or a hawkish Fed surprise this week could snap sentiment back quickly.
Sources: TipRanks, Yahoo Finance, CNBC.
