IMF cuts 2026 global growth forecast to 3.0%, raises inflation outlook

Economics / Macro · July 2026 World Economic Outlook update
  • The IMF's July World Economic Outlook update projects 3.0% global growth in 2026, down 0.1 point from April — its second downgrade this year — before a rebound to 3.4% in 2027.
  • The Fund now expects headline global inflation to rise from 4.1% in 2025 to 4.7% in 2026, driven by war-linked energy and food costs, before easing to 3.9% in 2027.
  • World trade growth is forecast to slow from 5.0% in 2025 to 3.5% in 2026 as tariffs and shipping disruption bite, recovering to 4.3% in 2027.
  • The IMF notes an uneven impact: energy exporters and AI-technology producers are faring better, while energy-importing nations face the sharpest headwinds.
  • The report explicitly cites Middle East conflict spillover and stalled disinflation as the swing factors — directly tying the geopolitical and rates stories in today's brief to the growth outlook.
Sources: IMF World Economic Outlook, Yahoo Finance, BigGo Finance.
