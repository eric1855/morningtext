Microsoft, Meta earnings test market's patience with AI spending

Equities · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • Microsoft reports fiscal Q4 after the close; analysts model EPS of roughly $4.22-$4.24 on revenue of $87.5-$87.7B, per consensus tracked by eciks.org and TipRanks.
  • Meta reports Q2 the same afternoon, call at 4:30pm ET; consensus EPS is roughly $7.18-$7.24, per Barchart and TipRanks.
  • Meta enters the print having raised full-year capex guidance to $125-$145B to fund AI infrastructure and data centers — the single biggest swing factor for the stock's reaction.
  • Azure growth and whether AI infrastructure spend is finally converting to monetized revenue (versus pure margin compression) is the key Microsoft debate, per Bloomberg.
  • Context from last week: Alphabet beat on most metrics but suffered its worst single-day stock drop in over a year after reporting negative free cash flow for the first time as a public company, driven by capex — a cautionary precedent hanging over today's prints.
  • Why it matters: two of the four largest US companies report hours after the Fed decision, on top of a deepening chip selloff — this is the highest-stakes 24-hour window of the quarter for AI-capex sentiment.
Sources: Bloomberg, Barchart, TipRanks, Fortune, eciks.org.
