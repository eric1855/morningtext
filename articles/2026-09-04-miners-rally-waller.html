GDX jumps 3.95% to $101.49, GDXJ +3.34% as Waller's dovish tilt fires up the miners

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Thursday, September 3, 2026
  • VanEck Gold Miners ETF (GDX) closed Thursday at $101.49, up 3.95%, its second straight double-digit-magnitude daily percentage move this week and a fresh high for the 2026 rally, after Fed Governor Christopher Waller said Thursday morning he would support holding rates steady at the September meeting if disinflation continues.
  • VanEck Junior Gold Miners ETF (GDXJ) closed at $132.33, up 3.34% ($4.28), broadly tracking the senior-producer move rather than showing the outsized negative-leverage swings that hit the junior names on Tuesday's rate-hike scare.
  • The move is classic negative-real-rate leverage: Waller's comments cut implied September hike odds from roughly 70% to 50% and knocked the dollar and the 10-year lower, and gold's own rally (bullion up more than 2% on the day) got amplified several times over in the producer and developer equities that make up EMA's core book.
  • Why it matters to EMA: this is the mining-equity mechanism the fund is built around — GDX has now decisively out-run bullion's own advance since the July low, and a dovish rate reset is exactly the kind of catalyst that widens that gap further.
