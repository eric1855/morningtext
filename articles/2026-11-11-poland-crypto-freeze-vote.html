Poland's Parliament Votes 240-202 to Let Officials Freeze Crypto Before Court Review

Regulation & Policy / Asset Seizure · Tuesday, November 10, 2026, ~3:20pm ET
  • Poland's Parliament (Sejm) passed, by a 240-202 vote, legislation granting officials the power to freeze crypto assets before a court review takes place.
  • The measure is a single-country move rather than an EU-wide or US CLARITY Act-level event, but it's an incremental data point on how pre-judicial asset-freeze powers are spreading into crypto-specific statute in major jurisdictions.
  • Why it matters to BOF: relevant context for custody and jurisdiction-of-operation decisions, even though it carries no direct read-through to US regulatory timelines.
Source: Bitcoin Magazine.
