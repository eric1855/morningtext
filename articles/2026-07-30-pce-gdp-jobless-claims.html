Core PCE, Q2 GDP and jobless claims all land at 8:30am ET

Economics / Macro · Thursday, July 30, 2026
  • The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, Core PCE, arrives alongside the advance estimate of Q2 GDP and weekly jobless claims — an unusually data-dense morning barely 18 hours after Wednesday's hawkish Fed hold.
  • Core PCE hit 3.4% y/y in May, its highest since October 2023; consensus for June sits in a 3.3-3.4% band with a slight-easing call the modal expectation.
  • Q2 GDP growth is expected around +2.3% annualized, up from +2.1% previously.
  • Initial jobless claims for the week ending July 25 are forecast near 201,000, versus 187,000 previously.
  • Why it matters: landing just after a Fed that dissented toward a hike, a hot Core PCE print would validate the hawkish tilt and could extend the Treasury selloff; a soft print would be the one release capable of reversing Wednesday's damage.
Sources: Investing.com, PIPTheory, BEA, Markets Today.
