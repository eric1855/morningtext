Consumer confidence sinks to 90.8, missing estimates

Economics / Macro · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • The Conference Board's Consumer Confidence Index fell 1.4 points to 90.8 in July, down from an upwardly revised 92.2 in June, per Bloomberg and the Conference Board.
  • The reading missed economist expectations of 92.4, per multiple outlets including Advisor Perspectives.
  • The Present Situation Index — consumers' view of current business and labor conditions — fell 3.6 points to 114.9, its third straight monthly decline.
  • The Expectations Index held flat at 74.7, still well below the 80 threshold historically associated with recession risk.
  • Consumers cited rising concern over the labor market alongside persistently elevated grocery and gas prices.
  • Why it matters: a softening consumer heading into a Fed decision and the heart of earnings season adds a genuine growth-scare variable the desk should track alongside the AI-capex and rate-path debates.
Sources: Bloomberg, The Conference Board, Advisor Perspectives, Yahoo Finance.
