Core PCE lands Thursday, right after the Fed decision

Economics / Macro · Monday, July 27, 2026
  • The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, Core PCE, is due Thursday, July 30 at 8:30am ET — one day after the FOMC's Wednesday rate decision, per financecalendar.com.
  • It won't move Wednesday's call, but it is among the last major inflation prints officials see before the September meeting, and will anchor near-term hike/cut odds either way.
  • The most recent Core PCE annual reading stood at 3.4%, up from 3.3% the prior month — still well above the Fed's 2% target, per Trading Economics.
  • Why it matters: a hot print Thursday would validate this week's hike-odds repricing even if the Fed holds Wednesday; a cool print would support the "one-and-done" oil-shock narrative and could re-open cut expectations for later this year.
Sources: financecalendar.com, Trading Economics.
