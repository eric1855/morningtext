Iran pause holds but stays fragile; Trump warns strikes could resume

Geopolitical · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • The US-Iran pause in strikes continued into a second/third day; Trump said the US halted new strikes at Iran's request and warned attacks would resume without a durable ceasefire deal, per CNN's live coverage.
  • Iran's government has said it is not currently in negotiations with the US, keeping the diplomatic path unresolved even as the guns stay quiet, per CNN.
  • Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz remains far below normal — fewer than 10 commodity vessels transited as of Monday versus roughly 100 on a typical pre-war day, a sign traders are not yet pricing a durable resolution.
  • Analysts note neither side has a clear path to a decisive win: Iran cannot meaningfully threaten US forces, and Washington cannot force Tehran's hand militarily — an argument for a grinding stalemate rather than quick resolution either way.
  • Why it matters: oil and the 10-year yield have both rallied/eased on de-escalation hopes over the past two sessions, but a fragile, unresolved pause means any headline reversal can snap the energy-price relief trade just as fast as it arrived.
Sources: CNN, House of Commons Library.
