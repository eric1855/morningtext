Iran war escalates as US resumes strikes overnight

Geopolitical · Thursday, July 30, 2026
  • US Central Command said it completed a "heavy wave" of overnight strikes on dozens of targets tied to Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps, including command centers, missile and drone facilities, and coastal defense sites.
  • The retaliation follows Tehran's missile attack on a US military base; President Trump had vowed the US would "hit Iran hard" in response.
  • Iran's IRGC said Thursday it "will punish the aggressor today," and warned that countries assisting the US would also face a "harsh response."
  • The conflict is widening: Egypt and Saudi Arabia are increasingly drawn into regional hostilities alongside earlier reports of joint US-Saudi strikes on Iran-backed forces in Iraq.
  • Brent crude jumped to roughly $92-93/bbl and WTI above $84 on the news, reversing the prior session's partial retreat.
  • Why it matters: a second consecutive night of strikes signals this is not a one-off reprisal — it raises the odds of a sustained conflict that keeps oil, gold and volatility bid while pressuring risk assets already nursing a hawkish-Fed hangover.
Sources: NBC News, CNBC, NPR, Bloomberg, CNN.
