Wheaton Precious Metals (WPM) Moved Down 5.11% on Nov 10

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Tuesday, November 10, 2026, ~1:15pm ET
  • Wheaton Precious Metals Corp (WPM) shares fell 5.11% in Tuesday trading, roughly double spot gold's own ~2.6-2.8% decline the same session, per a TradingKey stock-move report.
  • The move tracked the day's broader precious-metals selloff, triggered by a hotter-than-expected October CPI print (headline +0.4%, core +0.3%) and Fed Governor Christopher Waller's comments backing a December rate hike, which pushed implied hike odds toward 88-96% and sent the 10-year Treasury yield up 12.76bps to 4.988%.
  • Wheaton is a top-tier streaming/royalty name and tends to be more bullion-correlated than EMA's producer/developer/junior-driller-heavy book, but its leveraged downside move Tuesday mirrors the leveraged upside move the mining-equity complex showed on Monday's rally — the same operating-leverage mechanism cutting both ways.
  • Why it matters to EMA: a reminder that the mining-equity overweight that outperforms bullion on the way up will also underperform it on the way down when rate expectations move sharply against the sector, as they did Tuesday.
Sources: TradingKey stock-move report; Kitco AM/PM reports; USA Today market data feed.
