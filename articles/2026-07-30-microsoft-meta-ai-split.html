AI trade splits Big Tech: Microsoft jumps ~9%, Meta sinks ~9%

Equities · Thursday, July 30, 2026
  • Microsoft beat on fiscal Q4 revenue with Azure growing 43% y/y, crossing $100B in annualized revenue for the first time; Microsoft 365 Copilot topped 30 million paid seats.
  • Shares rose about 2.4% in Wednesday after-hours trade and extended to roughly +9% Thursday as investors credited Microsoft's AI capex with actually converting to revenue.
  • Meta's EPS came in at $6.18 versus roughly $7.22 expected; expenses rose 55% to $42B while revenue grew a slower 28%, and net income fell 14% to $15.8B.
  • Meta raised the low end of its 2026 capex guide to $130-145B (from $125-145B), deepening investor anxiety about payback timelines on AI spend.
  • Meta shares slumped roughly 6% in Wednesday's after-hours session and were down about 9% by Thursday.
  • Why it matters: the divergence is the market's clearest read yet on which hyperscalers are monetizing AI capex versus burning cash on it — a split that will shape positioning ahead of Apple and Amazon's reports tonight.
Sources: CNBC, Bloomberg, Axios, Euronews, Money Morning.
