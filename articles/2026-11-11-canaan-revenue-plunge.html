Canaan's Q3 Revenue Plunges 90% to $14.5 Million as Bitcoin Mining Hardware Sales Slump

Bitcoin Miners & Infrastructure / Hardware Demand · Tuesday, November 10, 2026, ~11:02am ET
  • Bitcoin mining-rig maker Canaan reported Q3 revenue of $14.5 million, a roughly 90% year-over-year collapse, as hardware sales slumped. A related same-day report noted Canaan separately swung to an $8 million profit on bitcoin gains even as rig sales fell 96%.
  • The results land amid a broader miner margin squeeze: hashprice has fallen to roughly $31.89/PH/s versus a $35.73 30-day average, and network hashrate/difficulty growth has slowed.
  • Why it matters to BOF: collapsing rig demand is a leading indicator that miners are deferring capex rather than expanding — consistent with the margin-pressure backdrop that eventually produces the distressed miner-debt opportunities BOF's mandate targets.
Source: The Block.
