10-year yield eases to 4.64% as oil retreats and the Fed meeting opens

Markets / Rates · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • The 10-year Treasury yield held around 4.64% Tuesday, down from 4.69% at Friday's close, as investors awaited the Fed's policy decision, per Trading Economics and YCharts.
  • The move follows oil's sharp retreat since the Iran pause began, easing near-term inflation pressure that had been pushing yields — and Fed hike odds — higher through mid-July.
  • Futures still price a roughly 36% chance of a 25bp hike Wednesday, so the yield move reflects incremental easing in inflation risk rather than a settled outlook.
  • Why it matters: lower yields are a modest tailwind for rate-sensitive equities even as chip stocks sell off — watch whether yields keep drifting lower into Wednesday's decision or snap back on any Iran or inflation-data surprise.
Sources: Trading Economics, YCharts, CME Group.
