Oil whipsaws as Mideast fighting flares, settles back near $82

Commodities / Geopolitical · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • WTI spiked toward $93.82 on fresh Middle East fighting before reversing sharply to a low near $77.97, then recovering to trade around $82, per fxdailyreport.com's July 29 analysis.
  • The US military said it intercepted a surprise Iranian attack targeting US troops stationed across the Middle East, per Bloomberg's oil-markets coverage.
  • Iran-backed militias in Iraq launched drone strikes on oil facilities in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Region for a second consecutive day.
  • Traders are also watching the OPEC meeting, weekly API crude inventory data, and today's Fed decision as near-term volatility drivers.
  • The break below oil's recent uptrend line suggests the rally that ran through mid-July may be reversing, though the path remains highly headline-dependent.
  • Why it matters: oil's retreat has been a disinflation tailwind supporting the case for a Fed hold — a renewed sustained spike on further Mideast escalation would flip that calculus quickly.
Sources: Bloomberg, fxdailyreport.com, Fortune.
