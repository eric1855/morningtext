AI/chip selloff deepens, Nasdaq nears correction territory

Equities / Markets · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • Intel dropped nearly 6% and AMD lost about 8% in Wednesday trading as the semiconductor rout extended for a fifth session, per CNBC.
  • Memory names were hit hardest: Micron and Seagate each lost more than 8%, Western Digital fell nearly 7%, and SanDisk shed roughly 14%.
  • SK Hynix's US-listed shares dropped 9%, and its Seoul-listed stock fell more than 15% after missing analyst estimates despite posting a record quarterly profit.
  • SoftBank, a major AI proxy via its Arm stake, lost nearly 7%, extending a decline that has taken the stock down almost 50% from its year-to-date high, per CNBC and Invezz.
  • Drivers cited: renewed progress in Chinese advanced chipmaking (including memory maker CXMT's blockbuster Shanghai debut) and deepening investor doubts about the sustainability of AI capital spending.
  • The Nasdaq Composite is being dragged toward correction territory as the chip-led decline broadens beyond pure semiconductor names into AI-adjacent plays.
  • Why it matters: this is the backdrop Microsoft and Meta report into this afternoon — any hint of slowing AI capex discipline could accelerate the rout further.
Sources: CNBC, Bloomberg, Invezz, Yahoo Finance.
