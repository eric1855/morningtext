Trump opens Section 301 probe into EU, threatens new tariffs

Geopolitical / Trade · Friday, July 24, 2026
  • Trump announced the US will investigate EU trade practices, alleging Brussels has unfairly fined US tech giants — Google, Apple, Meta and Amazon — via digital antitrust enforcement, per Bloomberg and the Washington Post.
  • The trigger: the EU hit Google with an €890M ($1B) fine a day earlier for allegedly favoring its own services in Google Play and search.
  • Trump invoked Section 301 of the Trade Act — his preferred legal route after the Supreme Court struck down his earlier sweeping tariffs in February — threatening a "big price" for the EU.
  • The move lands the same week 10%–12.5% tariffs on imports from 60 other trading partners took effect, per CNN Business, reviving the broader trade-war narrative markets had assumed was cooling.
  • For hedge fund positioning: this reopens tail risk on EU-exposed multinationals and adds another inflationary input alongside oil, complicating the Fed's rate path.
Sources: Bloomberg, Washington Post, CNN Business.
