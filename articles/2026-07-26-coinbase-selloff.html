Coinbase tumbles toward $158 on crypto-market downturn

Equities / Single Names · Week of July 20, 2026
  • Coinbase (COIN) shares gained 0.7% for the week but tumbled 10.0% after Tuesday's close, finishing Friday at $158.29, per Seeking Alpha's weekly market report.
  • The stock is down roughly 68% from its 52-week high near $445 (July 2025) as crypto trading volumes and market cap have each fallen more than 20% quarter-over-quarter.
  • Q1 results showed EBITDA of $303M, missing estimates by 24%, on a 31% YoY revenue drop to $1.41B; net income swung to a $394M loss versus a $66M profit a year earlier.
  • 54% of Coinbase's revenue comes from transaction fees, making it highly levered to trading activity — which craters in a crypto bear market.
  • Some institutions have reaffirmed Outperform ratings despite the slide, betting on a product-pipeline and regulatory-clarity recovery, but near-term sentiment remains negative.
Sources: Seeking Alpha, NAI 500, TIKR.
