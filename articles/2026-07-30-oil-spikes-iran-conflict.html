Oil spikes toward $92 Brent as Iran conflict widens

Financial / Markets · Thursday, July 30, 2026
  • Brent crude touched roughly $92-93/bbl by early Thursday, up more than $3 on the day, after the US launched a fresh wave of strikes on Iran overnight.
  • WTI crude traded above $84/bbl; Wednesday's session alone saw crude jump as much as 7% intraday on the initial retaliation headlines.
  • The move reverses part of a recent pullback and puts Brent roughly $19 above year-ago levels.
  • Traders are again pricing tail risk to flows through the Strait of Hormuz given the conflict's expansion into Saudi and Egyptian involvement.
  • Why it matters: a sustained move back above $90 Brent complicates the inflation picture just as today's Core PCE print lands, and directly threatens the "disinflation is on track" narrative the market had been leaning on.
Sources: Fortune, Bloomberg, CNBC, Al Jazeera.
