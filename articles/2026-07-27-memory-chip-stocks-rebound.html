Memory chip stocks rebound, SK Hynix +6% pre-market

Equities · Monday, July 27, 2026
  • Memory names rebounded in pre-market trade Monday, led by SK Hynix's roughly 6% gain, per TradingKey.
  • Sentiment was helped by ChangXin Technology's STAR Market debut, which surged over 400%, and reports of a $950B AI-semiconductor partnership between South Korean manufacturers and US tech giants.
  • South Korea's government also signed an MOU with AMD to build a joint AI research center and open computing infrastructure, per state ministry announcements.
  • SK Hynix reports Q2 results Wednesday, with revenue expected near $57.6B and operating profit near a record 64.1 trillion KRW.
  • This follows a rough stretch: Micron led a sharp early-July drawdown (-13%) on AI-capex valuation fears, part of the same rout that hit SanDisk (-11%) and the broader Philadelphia Semiconductor Index last week.
  • Why it matters: BlackRock called the chip selloff overdone last week — today's pre-market bounce, alongside fresh AI-infrastructure deal flow, is an early tell on whether that call is playing out.
Sources: TradingKey, Seeking Alpha, Yahoo Finance.
