10-year yield hits highest since Jan 2025, could test 5%

Financial / Markets · Friday, July 24, 2026
  • The 10-year Treasury yield closed at 4.69% Friday July 24, after touching 4.71% Thursday — its fifth straight day of gains and the highest level since January 2025, per CNBC.
  • The move is being driven by surging oil prices (Brent above $95/barrel) on Iran-war supply fears, which is rekindling inflation concerns among bond investors.
  • CNBC reports strategists increasingly flag a test of 5% on the 10-year if oil and geopolitical risk stay elevated into the fall.
  • Higher long-end yields are pressuring equity valuations broadly and hitting rate-sensitive sectors (housing, small caps) hardest.
  • The 2-year yield sits at 4.33%, keeping the curve modestly positively sloped even as the long end backs up.
Sources: CNBC, Advisor Perspectives / dshort Treasury Yields Snapshot.
