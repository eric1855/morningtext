Trump's Section 301 probe into EU escalates over tech fines

Geopolitical / Trade · Monday, July 27, 2026
  • Trump said the US will "immediately" open a Section 301 investigation into the EU, accusing the bloc of "robbing" American tech companies via regulatory fines, including a recent $1B fine on Google, per CNBC and the Washington Times.
  • He has threatened a "substantial" new tariff on the 27-member bloc as an outcome of the probe.
  • This sits on top of the existing US-EU trade framework, under which most EU goods already face a 15% tariff rate, implemented July 1 after EU parliamentary and Council approval.
  • Why it matters: this reopens trade-war tail risk for EU-exposed multinationals just as the Fed weighs a hike on oil-driven inflation — a second inflationary/uncertainty vector layering onto the energy shock this week.
Sources: CNBC, Washington Times.
