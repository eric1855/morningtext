Coinbase jumps ~6% as rival exchanges BitMEX, BitMart shut down

Financial / Markets · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • Coinbase (COIN) was one of the session's biggest winners, rising nearly 6% per The Coin Republic's July 29 coverage.
  • The move followed announcements that exchanges BitMEX and BitMart plan to shut down, pushing volume toward larger, more established platforms including Coinbase, Gemini and Bullish.
  • Bitcoin itself was little changed, trading near $63,500-$64,000, roughly flat to up about 1% over 24 hours, per CoinGabbar and CoinStats.
  • Some coverage noted Bitcoin dipped about 2% ahead of the Fed decision before stabilizing, with today's FOMC meeting cited as the primary near-term driver of crypto sentiment.
  • Fear-leaning sentiment persists across the broader ~$2.28T crypto market even as BTC and ETH posted modest gains.
  • Why it matters: exchange-consolidation flows are a rare bright spot for crypto-adjacent equities on a day otherwise dominated by Fed and AI-capex anxiety — watch COIN as a sentiment bellwether into the 2pm decision.
Sources: The Coin Republic, CoinGabbar, CoinStats.
