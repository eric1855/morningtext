Oil tops $95/barrel as Houthis declare Saudi maritime embargo

Financial / Markets · Week of July 20, 2026
  • Brent crude has pushed past $95/barrel as the Iran war and widening Red Sea disruption threaten global crude supply chains.
  • The EIA sees Brent averaging $95.39/barrel for full-year 2026; Barclays' range is $100/barrel in its base case, rising to $105 if Strait of Hormuz navigation isn't normalized soon.
  • Houthi rebels have declared a maritime embargo against Saudi Arabia, and Saudi Arabia has struck back at Houthi positions in Yemen — adding a second flashpoint beyond the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Crude had already round-tripped from a post-war peak above $126/barrel on April 30 down more than 38% before this month's re-escalation reversed the decline.
  • The oil move is the direct transmission channel into this week's other top stories: rising Treasury yields, a more hawkish Fed, and the IMF's inflation-outlook upgrade.
Sources: EIA, Barclays research (via financial press), Al Jazeera.
