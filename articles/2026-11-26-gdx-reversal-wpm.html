GDX rallies 3.5% to $73.89 in a Thanksgiving-eve reversal, gaining about 8.8% from its intraday low

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Wednesday, November 25, 2026, 4:00pm ET close
  • GDX closed Wednesday at about $73.89, up roughly 3.5% from Tuesday's $71.37 close. The day's range was about $67.93-74.26, and it opened near $68.76 (Yahoo Finance, CNBC and Finviz quotes agree on the close).
  • From the post-PCE low to the close, the miners ETF gained about 8.8%. Gold, by comparison, rose about 5% from its $3,426 low (lowest since September 2025) to ~$3,598 (FXLeaders, FXStreet).
  • The 3x leveraged GDXU closed at $49.32 (+~10.8% in the regular session), which points to short-covering and forced de-risking that reversed within the session.
  • Wheaton Precious Metals closed up 5.15% on the day (TradingKey). Royalty and streaming names led, the usual pattern when investors return to the sector through its highest-quality names first.
  • Context: GDX is still -16.8% YTD (Yahoo) and ~37% below its $117.18 52-week high, and Finviz shows a 3-month decline of ~28.7%.
  • Why it matters to EMA: this is the first session in about a week where miners outperformed the metal on the way up. That suggests the operating leverage that has hurt EMA's book during the drawdown is starting to work in its favor. A one-day reversal on Thanksgiving-eve volume does not confirm a bottom, though. Friday is a half-day, with thin liquidity.
Sources: Yahoo Finance GDX/GDXU quotes; CNBC GDX quote; Finviz; TradingKey (WPM, Nov 25); FXLeaders and FXStreet gold reports, Nov 25-26, 2026.
