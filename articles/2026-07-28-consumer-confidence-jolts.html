Consumer confidence and JOLTS due today, hours before the Fed's meeting resumes

Macro · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • The Conference Board's Consumer Confidence index and the BLS JOLTS job-openings report are both due at 10am ET today, per Investing.com's economic calendar.
  • June's confidence reading had fallen to 91.2, missing expectations, with May revised sharply lower — a soft-labor-market signal watched closely into a live Fed meeting.
  • June JOLTS job openings had come in at 7.594 million, above forecasts and signaling the labor market remains tighter than the confidence data alone would suggest.
  • Neither release will move Wednesday's rate decision directly since it lands mid-meeting, but a weak confidence print or a soft openings number would reinforce the case for a hold and further compress hike odds; a hot pair would do the opposite.
  • Why it matters: this is the last hard economic data point before Wednesday's FOMC decision — it's the marginal input most likely to move Fed-dated futures pricing intraday.
Sources: Investing.com, Conference Board, BLS-referenced coverage.
