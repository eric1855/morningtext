Silver gets back above $41 after a sub-$40 break, and the gold/silver ratio falls toward ~86.6

Precious Metals · Wednesday, November 25, 2026, ~7:06pm ET (Kitco)
  • Kitco quoted spot silver at about $41.29/oz at 19:06 on Nov 25, up from $39.72 on Wednesday morning (Fortune, 6:45am ET). That is a gain of roughly 4% after Tuesday's break below $40. JM Bullion showed $41.22 at 1:52pm ET.
  • Gold was ~$3,574.70 on the same Kitco print (-0.37% on the day), so the gold/silver ratio fell to ~86.6, down from ~88.4 on Wednesday morning.
  • Silver rose more than gold on the rebound even though inflation came in hot (core PCE 3.3% vs 3.2% expected) and a December Fed hike stays priced in. Falling Treasury yields (10Y to ~4.82%) and a softer dollar (DXY ~103.7) did the work.
  • Why it matters to EMA: silver equities such as SilverTiger, and SIL more broadly, carry more leverage to the metal than gold producers. If the ratio keeps falling from the high-80s, silver developers can re-rate faster than GDX. If the ratio rises back toward 90, it would signal that Wednesday's move was only short-covering. No same-day SIL or SILJ close was confirmed for this brief.
Sources: Kitco spot quotes (Nov 25, 19:06); JM Bullion (Nov 25, 13:52 EST); Fortune (Nov 25, 6:45am ET); CNBC (Treasury yields, Nov 25); Piq Markets (dollar, Asia Nov 26).
