Big Tech earnings collide with the chip rout: MSFT, META Wed; AAPL, AMZN Thu

Equities · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • Microsoft and Meta report after Wednesday's close; Apple and Amazon follow Thursday afternoon — four of the world's most valuable companies inside a 48-hour window, per AskTraders and TradingKey.
  • Microsoft: focus is on Azure growth versus a 36% "bogey" investors have set, against management's 39-40% constant-currency guide, and whether AI infrastructure spend is finally monetizing rather than just compressing margins.
  • Meta: watch advertising revenue growth, engagement, and AI-driven ad efficiency against the backdrop of sharply higher data-center and AI capex.
  • Amazon: consensus is roughly $196B in Q2 revenue and ~$1.82 EPS, about 17% top-line growth, within the 16-19% range Amazon guided to.
  • Capex guidance is the market's real pressure point: Alphabet's prior capex commentary already spooked investors worried about free-cash-flow drag from the AI buildout, per Fortune.
  • Why it matters: these prints land directly on top of a deepening AI-chip selloff — soft capex commentary from any of the four could extend the rout, while a strong Azure or ad-revenue beat could be the circuit-breaker the market needs.
Sources: AskTraders, TradingKey, Fortune, Yahoo Finance.
