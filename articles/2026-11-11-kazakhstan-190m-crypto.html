Kazakhstan Central Bank Invests $190M in Crypto Funds and Bitcoin ETFs

Institutional & Corporate / Sovereign Allocator · Tuesday, November 10, 2026, ~2:01pm ET
  • Kazakhstan's central bank disclosed a $190 million allocation into crypto funds and bitcoin ETFs, per a Cryptonews.net report landing in Tuesday afternoon's news flow.
  • The purchase came on a day spot bitcoin ETFs logged a fourth straight session of net outflows (-$95M, SoSoValue) and bitcoin sold off alongside gold and silver on hawkish Fed repricing — a structural, official-sector buyer stepping in against the day's headline flow direction.
  • Why it matters to BOF: sovereign and central-bank adoption runs on its own allocation timeline, independent of daily ETF flow prints or rate-driven selloffs, and is a demand data point worth tracking separately from short-term price action.
Source: Cryptonews.net.
