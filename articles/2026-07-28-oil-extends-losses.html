Oil extends losses as Iran pause holds — WTI near $80

Markets / Commodities · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • WTI crude for September delivery fell roughly 3% to about $80/barrel Tuesday; Brent slipped to the mid-$80s to high-$80s, per CNBC and Trading Economics.
  • The move extends Monday's sharp drop and follows the continued pause in US-Iran fighting, which has eased the war-risk premium built into crude since the conflict began.
  • Lower oil is a two-edged signal for markets: it eases the inflation impulse that had pushed FOMC hike odds higher, but it also reflects genuine geopolitical de-escalation the desk has to keep discounting for reversal risk.
  • Falling energy costs are also feeding into lower Treasury yields this week, a tailwind for rate-sensitive equities even as chip stocks sell off.
  • Why it matters: energy is currently the single biggest swing factor in both the inflation and equity-risk narrative — a re-escalation in the Strait of Hormuz would reverse this move fast, given shipping traffic still hasn't normalized.
Sources: CNBC, Trading Economics, Forbes Advisor.
