SanDisk plunges 11% on memory weakness

Equities · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • SanDisk shares dropped about 11% Monday as memory-market weakness weighed on the stock, even as the broader Dow rose on the day's oil retreat, per The Motley Fool.
  • The move sits alongside a broader memory-chip selldown that deepened into Tuesday, with Micron down nearly 5% and Asian memory makers SK Hynix and Samsung both down double digits.
  • The weakness contrasts with a memory-stock rebound narrative from just a session or two earlier, when BlackRock had called the prior chip selloff "overdone" — today's price action argues that call was premature, at least for memory names.
  • Why it matters: memory pricing is a leading indicator for AI-infrastructure capex cycles; a sustained SanDisk/Micron/SK Hynix drawdown would be a warning sign heading into this week's hyperscaler earnings on capex plans.
Sources: The Motley Fool, CNBC.
