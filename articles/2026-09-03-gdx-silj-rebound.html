GDX rebounds 3.1% to $97.63, SILJ recovers as miners bounce off Tuesday's rout

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Wednesday, September 2, 2026
  • VanEck Gold Miners ETF (GDX) closed Wednesday at $97.63, up 3.13% ($2.96), recovering roughly four-fifths of Tuesday's sharp -3.90% negative-leverage session that had outpaced bullion's own decline by nearly 4x.
  • Junior miners moved in step: Amplify Junior Silver Miners ETF (SILJ) climbed back toward the low-$30s after Tuesday's slide to $30.03, tracking silver's own bounce off the week's lows.
  • The rebound tracks a broader theme rather than a single catalyst: GDX has been the strongest debasement-trade vehicle of 2026, up roughly 52% off its July low on a wave of retail inflows (~$419M in August alone, the ETF's best monthly intake since February) and a 10.4x P/E that leaves miners still cheap versus bullion's own advance.
  • Why it matters to EMA: Wednesday's bounce confirms the negative-leverage move on Tuesday was a rate-hike-odds scare rather than a thesis break — miners are still trading with the operating leverage to gold that underpins the fund's overweight to producers and developers over bullion itself. Sizing any dip in the space should distinguish one-day rate repricings like Tuesday's from a genuine reversal in the gold uptrend.
Sources: VanEck, StockAnalysis.com, Benzinga.
