Iran war escalation nears two-week mark as ceasefire crumbles

Geopolitical · Saturday, July 25, 2026
  • The US bombing campaign against Iran has continued for roughly 13 consecutive nights after the earlier ceasefire agreement broke down, per CNN's live coverage of the conflict.
  • Iran's health ministry says 59 people have been killed and 666 injured since June 27; the Pentagon has confirmed at least four US service members killed in the latest wave of Iranian strikes.
  • US Central Command did not announce new strikes on Friday, a possible pause after nearly two weeks of nightly bombing — but officials on both sides continue to trade accusations of ceasefire violations, per CNN and Al Jazeera.
  • The US military says it disabled a tanker attempting to bypass its blockade on Iranian ports.
  • Saudi Arabia struck Yemen's Iran-backed Houthi rebels after the group attacked another Saudi vessel in the Red Sea, widening the shipping-lane risk beyond the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Barclays warns that if freedom of navigation through the Strait isn't restored soon, it expects Brent crude to average $105/barrel in 2026 (vs. $100 in its base case) — a direct line from this conflict to inflation and Fed policy.
Sources: CNN, Al Jazeera, Barclays (via financial press).
