Fed decision day arrives with hike odds near one-in-three

Macro / Rates · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • The FOMC's statement lands at 2:00pm ET today, with Chair Warsh's press conference at 2:30pm ET, capping the July 28-29 meeting.
  • CME FedWatch odds as of Tuesday morning showed roughly 68.5% probability of a hold at 3.50-3.75% versus 31.5% for a 25bp hike — a hold is the base case, but the hike tail is far from trivial.
  • Economists polled by FactSet lean more heavily toward a hold, which would mark a fifth straight meeting without a move; the gap between FactSet consensus and futures-implied odds reflects genuine market uncertainty.
  • Inflation risk from the Iran conflict (oil-driven CPI pressure) and AI-related capacity bottlenecks are the arguments cited for a surprise hike, per Yahoo Finance's Fed-meeting live coverage.
  • Treasury yields ticked up slightly into the decision — the 10-year added about a basis point to 4.61%, the 2-year rose to roughly 4.30% — consistent with a market leaning toward "hold" but hedging some hike risk.
  • Why it matters: a surprise hike would land on the same afternoon as Microsoft and Meta earnings and an already-fragile chip tape — the single biggest event-risk cluster of the week.
Sources: CBS News, Kiplinger, Yahoo Finance, MarketScreener, CME Group FedWatch-referenced coverage.
