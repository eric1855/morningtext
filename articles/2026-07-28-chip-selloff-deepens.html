Chip selloff deepens as China lithography claims rattle Wall Street

Equities / Markets · Tuesday, July 28, 2026
  • The semiconductor rout that started Monday extended into Tuesday: Nvidia fell roughly 5% Monday (about $250B in market cap in a single session) and was down another ~1.2% Tuesday, per Bloomberg and Yahoo Finance.
  • AMD dropped over 8% Monday and was off more than 3% again Tuesday; Intel and memory maker Micron each fell 3-5% Tuesday.
  • The selloff went global: SK Hynix slumped as much as 13-15%, Samsung Electronics fell up to 10%, dragging South Korea's Kospi down roughly 9% — a bellwether index for AI capex sentiment. Japan's Nikkei and Taiwan's benchmark each fell nearly 4%.
  • Trigger cited across outlets: a report that a Chinese firm cracked a lithography manufacturing step Western chipmakers believed they still controlled, layered on top of existing doubts about returns on AI infrastructure spending.
  • Jim Cramer flagged "monstrous" institutional selling behind the move rather than routine profit-taking, per 24/7 Wall St.; some analysts argue OpenAI-deal-related fears on Nvidia are overblown.
  • Why it matters: this is the backdrop Microsoft, Meta, Apple and Amazon report into this week — any soft AI-capex commentary from Big Tech could compound rather than calm the rout.
Sources: Bloomberg, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, 24/7 Wall St.
