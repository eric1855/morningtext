Bitcoin holds above $65K on risk-on unwind

Crypto / Markets · Monday, July 27, 2026
  • Bitcoin traded around $65,300-65,500 early Monday, holding a fourth consecutive weekly gain, per CoinDesk and Fortune.
  • Ether led crypto higher, with DeFi tokens Aave and Ondo among the session's top performers.
  • The move tracks the broader risk-on unwind as the US-Iran pause extends and Oman-hosted peace talks continue.
  • Why it matters: crypto's bid alongside equity futures confirms this morning's move is a genuine broad-based risk-on repricing, not just an index-futures technical bounce.
Sources: CoinDesk, Fortune.
