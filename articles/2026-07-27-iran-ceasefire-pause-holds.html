US-Iran pause holds into second day, oil craters ~7%

Geopolitical · Monday, July 27, 2026
  • The US paused strikes on Iran for a second straight day Sunday, and Tehran said it did the same, per NPR and CNBC.
  • The pause follows nearly two weeks of escalation sparked by Iranian fire on ships transiting the Strait of Hormuz; the US Navy's reimposed blockade continues, with a dozen commercial vessels redirected and two disabled.
  • Reports that Iran agreed to a 10-day ceasefire were denied by Tehran on Monday — the situation remains fluid and unconfirmed as a durable truce.
  • Pakistan, with Chinese backing, is reportedly working to revive US-Iran talks, per Bloomberg-sourced wire reports.
  • Brent crude tumbled more than 7% intraday, dipping below $90/barrel before settling near $92, on hopes the diplomatic pause holds — even as crude remains up roughly 8% for the week on the prior escalation.
  • Why it matters for the desk: this is the single largest swing factor in pre-market pricing — a genuine de-escalation unwinds the war-risk/oil premium that has been driving both equities and the 10-year yield for two weeks.
Sources: NPR, CNBC, Jerusalem Post, wire reports (Bloomberg-sourced).
