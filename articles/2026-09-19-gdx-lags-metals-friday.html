GDX slips 0.46% to $95.48 even as gold +1.0% and silver +3.0% extend their rebound

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Friday, September 18, 2026
  • VanEck Gold Miners ETF (GDX) closed Friday at $95.48, down 0.46% from Thursday's $95.92 close, even as spot gold rose roughly 1.0% to near $4,391/oz and spot silver rose 3.0% to $67.18/oz — both metals' third straight up session since Wednesday's hawkish-Fed-hike dip.
  • The divergence is the mirror image of Thursday's session, when GDX rallied 3.36% versus gold's same-day 1.02% move — more than 2x the metal's advance on the leverage embedded in producer and developer equities.
  • No single stock-specific catalyst explains Friday's pullback; it reads as a normal one-day give-back after two strong up sessions for the miners, plus some rotation as risk appetite broadly favored bitcoin and altcoins (BTC +5.4%, ETH +6.5%, SOL +12.7%) over precious metals on the day.
  • Why it matters to EMA: embedded operating leverage cuts both ways — the same mechanism that took GDX up more than 3x gold's move on Thursday can also produce a session where miners lag a rallying metal, as happened Friday with silver's best print of the week. It's a reminder that EMA's producer/developer selection is meant to trade around this dispersion rather than simply track GDX passively through it.
Sources: VanEck, Investing.com, Yahoo Finance, USAGOLD.
