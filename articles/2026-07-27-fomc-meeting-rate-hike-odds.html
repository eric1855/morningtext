FOMC meets Tue-Wed as hike odds jump to 38% from 12% a week ago

Macro / Rates · Monday, July 27, 2026
  • The Fed's two-day meeting runs July 28-29, with the decision due Wednesday afternoon; the fed funds target range currently stands at 3.50-3.75%, per CBS News and Forbes.
  • Prediction-market odds of a 25bp hike have surged to roughly 38% (some venues show ~19-36%), up from just 12% a week earlier, as oil-driven inflation fears built through the Iran conflict.
  • Consensus still leans toward a hold (60-80% depending on venue), but the dispersion itself signals how unsettled the rate path has become.
  • New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh has dropped explicit forward guidance, declining to signal the committee's lean — adding to uncertainty into the decision.
  • June CPI and PPI had cooled more than expected, giving the committee some cover to wait-and-see even as energy-driven headline inflation risk resurfaces.
  • Why it matters: today's oil-driven relief rally could reduce hike odds intraday, but a still-live 25bp hike risk into Wednesday is a genuine tail case the desk should not be pricing at zero.
Sources: CBS News, Forbes, HNGN, Polymarket-referenced odds.
