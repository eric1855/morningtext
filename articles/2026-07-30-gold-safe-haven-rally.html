Gold rallies on safe-haven bid as Middle East war escalates

Financial / Markets · Thursday, July 30, 2026
  • Gold rose more than 1% Wednesday as escalating US strikes on Iran and heightened geopolitical uncertainty drove renewed safe-haven demand.
  • The move comes after a volatile stretch for the metal this year — gold is well off its January record near $5,595 after a roughly 25% drawdown earlier in the conflict.
  • The bid coincides with a firmer dollar and a jump in equity volatility as investors digest the Fed's hawkish hold alongside the renewed strikes.
  • Why it matters: gold's response is a cleaner read on genuine fear versus the more crowded and policy-sensitive Treasury market — a sustained breakout would confirm the Iran conflict, not just the Fed, is now the dominant risk driver.
Sources: Yahoo Finance, Canadian Mining Report, AEI.
