FTSE 100 Closes 31 Points Lower as Gold Miners Slide

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Tuesday, November 10, 2026, ~12:16pm ET
  • London's FTSE 100 closed 31 points lower Tuesday, with London-listed gold miners among the session's weakest names, per a Share Talk market wrap.
  • The decline tracked the same catalyst hitting miners globally: a hot October US CPI print and hawkish comments from Fed Governor Waller pushed December rate-hike odds to roughly 88-96%, driving spot gold down ~2.6-2.8% and silver down ~3-4% on the day.
  • Why it matters to EMA: the UK tape confirms Tuesday's miner weakness was a global, rate-driven move rather than a single-market or single-name quirk — relevant for a book with cross-listed producer, developer, and royalty names.
Sources: Share Talk market wrap; Kitco AM/PM reports.
