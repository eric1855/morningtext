Treasury yields tick up as Wall Street awaits the Fed

Rates · Wednesday, July 29, 2026
  • The 10-year Treasury yield rose about one basis point to 4.614% on Wednesday morning, per MarketScreener and CNBC.
  • The 2-year yield advanced roughly two basis points to about 4.30%, a modest bear-flattening move into the decision.
  • Tuesday's 10-year close was roughly 4.61-4.62%, itself down from Monday's 4.64% close, per multiple sources including tradingeconomics.com and FRED-referenced coverage.
  • CNBC's Wednesday coverage frames the modest yield uptick as traders squaring positions ahead of the 2pm ET statement rather than a directional bet.
  • Why it matters: the move is small, but a "hold" outcome broadly matches current pricing — a surprise hike would likely send yields sharply higher and pressure equity multiples further into an already fragile tape.
Sources: CNBC, MarketScreener, tradingeconomics.com.
