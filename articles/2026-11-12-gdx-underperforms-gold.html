VanEck Gold Miners ETF (GDX) Closes Down 1.48% Wednesday, Underperforming Gold's ~1.2% Drop

Precious Metals / Mining Equities · Wednesday, November 11, 2026, 4:00pm ET close
  • GDX closed Wednesday at $85.21, down $1.28 (-1.48%) from Tuesday's $86.49 close, per Yahoo Finance and StockAnalysis.com data, on a session when Kitco's PM report had spot gold slipping below $4,000/oz (roughly -1.2% on the day) as a stronger dollar and firming December Fed-hike bets pressured bullion.
  • The miners' ETF fell at roughly 1.2x gold's own decline — the same operating-leverage pattern EMA's book has shown all week, amplifying both the Monday rally and this complex's down days since the hot October CPI print flipped the rate debate from cuts to hikes.
  • Silver held up somewhat better than gold in the same session per Kitco's commentary, which is a data point worth watching for EMA's producer/developer mix: if silver's relative resilience persists, SIL-type silver-miner exposure could start to diverge from GDX's gold-miner complex.
  • Why it matters to EMA: GDX is the fund's own stated benchmark, and a third straight session of miner underperformance versus bullion is the clearest read yet that the mining-equity premium is compressing alongside the broader debasement-trade pause, not just a single-name (Wheaton) effect from Tuesday.
Sources: Yahoo Finance GDX quote; StockAnalysis.com GDX historical close; Kitco PM Report, Nov 11, 2026.
