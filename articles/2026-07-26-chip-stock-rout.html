AI memory-chip rout spreads from Korea to US

Equities / Single Names · Friday, July 24, 2026
  • South Korea's Kospi plunged 5.7% Friday, with SK Hynix down about 11% and Samsung Electronics down nearly 8%, before the selloff spilled into US trading, per Yahoo Finance / 24/7 Wall St.
  • SanDisk closed down more than 10%, Micron fell around 6%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index tumbled over 4% — briefly entering bear-market territory intraday.
  • The proximate trigger: growing investor skepticism that hyperscalers' ballooning AI capex (see Alphabet's guidance hike) is translating into near-term returns, reviving valuation concerns across the memory-chip complex.
  • Context matters: even after the drop, Micron is up 227% YTD, SanDisk up 526% YTD, and Western Digital up 209% YTD — this is a sharp pullback inside a still-massive AI-cycle rally, not a reversal of the trend.
  • BlackRock strategists called the rout "overdone" and flagged Micron and SanDisk as buying opportunities on weakness (see companion item).
Sources: Yahoo Finance, 24/7 Wall St., BigGo Finance.
