Tesla, Alphabet lose hundreds of billions on AI-capex fears

Equities / Single Names · Thursday, July 23, 2026
  • Alphabet and Tesla both beat revenue estimates for Q2 but shed a combined several hundred billion dollars in market value the day after reporting, per HNGN and The Motley Fool.
  • Alphabet posted revenue of $119.8B (vs. $117B expected) and EPS of $2.26 (vs. $2.24 expected); Google Cloud revenue hit $24.8B and Search grew 17%.
  • Shares fell anyway after Alphabet raised its 2026 capex guidance to $195B–$205B, up from a prior $180B–$190B range, deepening investor unease about hyperscaler AI spending discipline.
  • Tesla posted an earnings miss with operating expenses outgrowing revenue; the stock suffered one of its sharpest single-day declines in years.
  • The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.15% on the reaction day (to 25,137.69) as the "profit vs. spending" debate around AI infrastructure intensified — a theme likely to dominate the rest of earnings season.
Sources: HNGN, The Motley Fool, CNBC.
